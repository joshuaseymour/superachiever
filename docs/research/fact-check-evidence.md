## Counterexamples to "NONE combine home and work"

### Sunsama
- **Weekly Planning Ritual**: ✅ Yes, built-in
- **Home+Work Unified**: ✅ "Set weekly objectives, turn them into daily homework or home-work tasks"
- **Calendar Integration**: ✅ Google, Outlook, iCloud
- **Source**: help.sunsama.com/docs/usage-guides/weekly-objectives/weekly-planning/

### Akiflow
- **Weekly Planning Ritual**: ✅ Yes, "Weekly Planning" and "Weekly Shutdown" built into rituals
- **Home+Work Unified**: ✅ "Work & Life" tagline, "Work & Personal, in perfect sync"
- **Calendar Integration**: ✅ Multiple calendars
- **Source**: akiflow.com, product.akiflow.com/help/articles/0805246-rituals

### Motion
- **Weekly Planning Ritual**: ✅ Yes, "Weekly planning task" can be set as recurring
- **Home+Work Unified**: ✅ "Combine all your calendars - work or personal"
- **Calendar Integration**: ✅ Google, Outlook, iCloud
- **Source**: usemotion.com

### Reclaim.ai
- **Weekly Planning Ritual**: ✅ Yes, AI Planner organizes week
- **Home+Work Unified**: ✅ "designed to account for both work and personal commitments"
- **Calendar Integration**: ✅ Google, Outlook
- **Source**: reclaim.ai

### Amie
- **Weekly Planning Ritual**: ⚠️ Daily and weekly overviews, but not a structured ritual
- **Home+Work Unified**: ✅ "Personal and work Google accounts" in one view
- **Calendar Integration**: ✅ Multiple Google accounts
- **Source**: amie.so

### Morgen
- **Weekly Planning Ritual**: ⚠️ Has weekly view, AI suggestions, but not a structured ritual
- **Home+Work Unified**: ✅ "Manage your work and personal commitments in one place"
- **Calendar Integration**: ✅ Google, Outlook, iCloud, Fastmail
- **Source**: morgen.so

### Notion Family Templates
- **Weekly Planning Ritual**: ⚠️ Can be built, not pre-structured
- **Home+Work Unified**: ✅ "This Week's Focus", "Family Calendar", person-specific scheduling
- **Calendar Integration**: ✅ Can integrate calendars
- **Source**: Multiple Notion templates (Family Command Center, Family & Household Command Center)
