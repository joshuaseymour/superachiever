## The Product

One page filled in together every Sunday (~30 min) by whoever shares your week. Three named results at top (mark moved/not moved). Where things stand (home + work, 2 min). Next moves with names (whose move next). What moved since last week. **Fall 2026:** $18K/60 days, day-30 refund if attended all 4 Sundays and none of 3 results moved. **Jan 2027:** Maybe $29-99/mo self-serve via ads.

---

## How to Make It Last Past Week 3

**Median habit formation: 66 days** (range 18-254, N=96, Lally 2010). Missing one day: -0.29 points (small). Missing a week: significantly reduces likelihood. Productivity apps: median retention 15% day-7, 8% day-30. Week 2-4 is the cliff. Design must celebrate week 4, forgive one miss, escalate at two.

**Key rules:** (1) Anchor to existing Sunday routine (implementation intentions d=0.27-0.66). (2) Target 20-30 min (Gottman 20 min/week). (3) Make anticipation/monitoring explicit (women 73% cognitive labor, visibility reduces conflict). (4) Assign complete ownership, not subtasks. (5) Week 1 must deliver fast value.

## Appendix: Verified Claims with Source Excerpts

### Habit Formation

**Lally et al. (2010), N=96:**  
- Median 66 days to automaticity: "average time (among those for whom our model was a good fit) was 66 days" (UCL news release)
- Range 18-254 days: "ranged from 18 to 254 days" (Lally abstract)
- Missing one day: "automaticity decreased by an average of 0.29 points on the SRHI automaticity subscale; a very small decrease"
- Missing a week: "missing a week's worth of opportunities reduces the likelihood of future performance and hinders habit acquisition"
- **Source:** https://doi.org/10.1002/ejsp.674

**Sheeran et al. (2025), 642 tests:**  
- Implementation intentions d=0.27-0.66: "Across 642 independent tests, forming implementation intentions proved effective for cognitive, affective, and behavioural outcomes (.27 ≤ d ≤ .66)"
- **Source:** https://doi.org/10.1080/10463283.2024.2334563

**App retention (UXCam 2026, AppsFlyer 28B+ installs):**  
- Productivity apps day-7: "Day-7 retention | 22-28% | 15%" (strong/median)
- Productivity apps day-30: "Day-30 | 12-18% | 8%" (strong/median)
- **Source:** https://uxcam.com/blog/mobile-app-retention-benchmarks/

### Mental Load & Cognitive Labor

**Aviv et al. (2024), USC study, N=322 mothers:**  
- 73% cognitive labor: "mothers reported being responsible for about 73% [72.57%] of all cognitive household labor compared with their partners' 27% [27.43%]"
- 64% physical labor: "64% [63.64%] of all physical household labor compared with their partners' 36%"
- Outcomes: "Greater cognitive burden associated with higher depression, stress, burnout, poorer mental health, and lower relationship quality"
- **Source:** https://doi.org/10.1007/s00737-024-01490-w

**Daminger (2019), 70 interviews with 35 couples:**  
- "Using 70 in-depth interviews with members of 35 couples, this study argues..." (Daminger abstract)
- Cognitive labor division analyzed in 32 couples (not all 35): "in 26 of the 32 different-sex couples I interviewed, the female partner completed more total cognitive labor" (81%)
- **Source:** https://doi.org/10.1177/0003122419859007

**USC Fair Play intervention (2024):**  
- Attrition: "47 out of 142 (33.1%) completed the intervention across both groups" (67% attrition)
- **Source:** USC Public Exchange (2024), https://public-exchange.org/usc/wp-content/uploads/sites/2/2025/09/PX_FairPlay-Final-Report_Dec2024-2.pdf

### Gottman & Weekly Check-Ins

**Gottman State of the Union (practitioner guidance, not RCT):**  
- 20 min/week minimum: Gottman Institute (2024), https://www.gottman.com/blog/how-to-have-a-state-of-the-union-meeting/
- Seven Principles d=0.23 overall, d=0.62 clinical subgroup: Zahl-Olsen et al. (2024), observational study with propensity matching, N=490+242 control, https://doi.org/10.1111/jmft.12726

### SMART Goals Critique

**McEwan et al. (2016), meta-analysis, N=5,912:**  
- Specific vs. vague: "specific (k = 31, d = 0.589, 95% C.I. 0.43-0.75) and vague or unclear (k = 21, d = 0.511, 95% C.I. 0.33-0.70) goals" with no significant difference
- **Source:** https://doi.org/10.1080/17437199.2015.1104258
