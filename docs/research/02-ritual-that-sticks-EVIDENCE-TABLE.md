## Key Errors to Correct

### Authors/Years Wrong
1. **USC Fair Play study**: Authors are Aviv et al. (2024), NOT Levkovich et al. (2024)
2. **SMART goals critique**: McEwan et al. (2016), NOT 2021
3. **Scholz couples support**: Published 2018 (Berli et al.), not 2016

### Statistics Wrong
1. **Daminger 73%**: This figure is from Aviv et al. 2024 (USC), not Daminger 2019. Daminger gives no percentage.
2. **Gottman effect size**: d=0.23 overall (d=0.62 for clinical subgroup only), NOT d=0.4–0.6
3. **Gottman study design**: Observational with propensity score matching, NOT an RCT
4. **Commitment device smoking**: 3-5 percentage point improvement, NOT 52% quit rate

### Unverifiable Claims (No Source Found)
1. **Fair Play 61% more equal balance**: No such statistic in the report
2. **2024 habit formation meta-analysis**: No source provided or found
3. **Savings commitment 80% higher balances**: Cited but not verified in time
