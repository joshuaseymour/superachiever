# Gap Check: Two People, One Shared Weekly Page, Home + Work
## Research completed: October 2, 2026

**Claim tested**: "No product or service has two people filling in one shared weekly page together covering home and work."

**Research method**: Fetched official pages, app store listings, help docs, and feature pages for 29+ apps and services, plus templates and guides.

---

## Summary Verdict: **Gap Holds**

**Finding**: No product or service positions a recurring weekly session where both people sit down together to fill in one shared page/view that integrates home AND work planning as its primary deliverable.

**Strongest partial fills**:
1. **Tandem** (couples app with "Weekly Sync" feature) - Closest to the gap, but no evidence it covers work planning beyond shared calendar sync
2. **Huddle** (20-min weekly meeting app, waitlist only) - Positions the meeting itself, but unclear if it produces one shared artifact or covers work
3. **Notion weekly review templates** (user-created) - People build their own, some integrate work+home, but no built-in collaborative session feature

**What exists**:
- Apps for shared household calendars/tasks (home only)
- Apps for individual work planning (Sunsama, Akiflow)
- Apps for couples relationship check-ins (Paired, Lasting, Kindred, Connected)
- Templates and guides for weekly meetings (but you build it yourself in Notion/Google Docs)

**What doesn't exist**: A product where the weekly planning *session* (both people, ~30 minutes, one artifact, home + work integrated) is the core offering.

## Additional Context: Weekly Meeting Guides & Templates

Multiple blog posts and guides describe weekly couples/household planning meetings, but none are products—they're instructions for using generic tools (Notion, Google Docs, etc.):

### Found Guides:
1. **commitment.life** - "Couples Weekly Planning Meeting Guide" - Describes 20-30 min meeting with shared note: "appreciation, challenge from past week, upcoming schedule, practical agreement, connection plan"
2. **tandem-app.eu/guides** - "Weekly Couple Planning Meeting (30-Minute Template)" - 5 sections: wins, calendar, tasks, money, recap
3. **dontforgetme.app** - "Weekly Couples Check-In Template: 15 Minutes That Save Your Relationship" - 3 parts: logistics, emotions, balance
4. **goalsandprogress.com** - "Work-Life Balance for Dual-Career Couples" - Describes "Weekly Sync Meeting" (30-45 min): priorities, support needs, sustainability check
5. **katelynarterburn.com** - "Family Ops: Sharing our agenda for a successful Weekly Family Meeting" - Uses Notion for shared agenda, 25-45 min, covers appreciation, logistics, connection
6. **marishamanahova.com** - "The Home Weekly Review" - 30-60 min Sunday review: reflection, next week planning, food plan, financial overview
7. **LinkedIn post** (Johanna & partner, mestarimakeschampions) - "every Sunday, Johanna and I sit down for 1 hour and plan the week ahead" - "Every meeting on both our calendars, Which ones we're attending together, Kids' drop-offs, pick-ups, activities, Who's with our senior dog, Workout times"

**Key observation**: These guides describe the exact practice the gap identifies—two people, one shared view, weekly session, work + home—but they're DIY instructions, not productized. Users must:
1. Pick a tool (Notion, Google Docs, shared note app)
2. Create their own template
3. Remember to do the meeting
4. Maintain the system themselves

No one sells "the weekly session as a service" or "the collaborative planning app built around this specific ritual."

## Gap Analysis: What Would Fill It?

To fully fill the gap, a product would need ALL of these:

✅ **Recurring weekly session** - Not just a calendar or task list you can check anytime; a structured session you do together  
✅ **Both people together** - Not async (one person adds, the other views later); both sit down at the same time  
✅ **One shared page/artifact** - A single view both people look at and fill in during the session  
✅ **Home + work integrated** - Not "home only" (Fair Play, Cozi) or "work only" (Sunsama, Akiflow)  
✅ **The session is the product** - The recurring planning session is the primary deliverable, not just a feature

### Current products come close but miss one or more:
- **Fair Play/FairShare**: ✅ Weekly session, ✅ Both people, ✅ One shared page, ❌ Home only
- **Huddle**: ✅ Weekly session, ✅ Both people, ❌ Unclear if one artifact, ❌ Unclear if work+home, ✅ Session is product (waitlist only)
- **Tandem**: ✅ Weekly Sync, ✅ Both people implied, ❌ No clear single "page", ❌ Appears home-focused despite calendar sync
- **Haklo**: ✅ Weekly meeting, ✅ Both people, ✅ Structured flow, ❌ Family/home only
- **Notion templates**: ✅ Can be built to cover all, ❌ Not a product; you build it yourself, no facilitation

## Strongest Counterexamples (Partial Fills)

### 1. **Tandem** (tandem-app.eu)
**What it has**: Shared todos, shared calendar, shared finances, and a "Weekly Sync" feature for couples  
**Quote**: "Plan the week, share responsibilities, stay on top of money, and make time for each other. Tandem keeps everyday life together without turning your relationship into project management."  
**Why it's close**: Explicitly has a "Weekly Sync" and integrates calendar (can sync external work calendars), tasks, and finances  
**Why it doesn't fill the gap**: 
- No evidence the Weekly Sync produces "one page" both people fill in together
- Appears to focus on home/relationship logistics; work planning not explicitly positioned
- The feature description doesn't center on "both people in a 30-min session filling in one shared artifact"

**Verdict**: Closest app-based partial fill, but still primarily couples/home-focused

---

### 2. **Huddle** (letshuddle.co, waitlist only)
**What it has**: Positions the 20-minute weekly planning session as its core product  
**Quote**: "Twenty minutes a week to share what you're each carrying, name what matters, and walk into the week on the same team." "Before the Huddle, each of you takes five minutes alone. How full is your tank? What's on your plate? What feels heaviest?"  
**Why it's close**: The session itself IS the product; both people participate; weekly cadence  
**Why it doesn't fill the gap**:
- Waitlist only; no public feature details on whether it creates "one shared page/artifact"
- Unclear if it covers work planning or primarily relationship/household
- Emphasis on "what you're each carrying" suggests mental/emotional load more than integrated work+home planning

**Verdict**: Could be a full fill if it (a) creates one shared planning artifact and (b) explicitly covers work + home, but evidence insufficient

---

### 3. **Notion Weekly Review Templates** (user-created)
**What exists**: Many couples/households create their own weekly review templates in Notion that cover work + home  
**Example (katelynarterburn.com)**: "We keep everything in Notion. Both of us can add topics throughout the week, and it's all there when we sit down on Friday. Before Notion, we used a Google Doc."  
**Why it's close**: Can be exactly the practice described—both people, one shared page, weekly session, work + home  
**Why it doesn't fill the gap**:
- Not a product; it's a DIY approach using general-purpose software
- No built-in facilitation, reminders, or structure specific to the weekly session
- Users must create and maintain it themselves

**Verdict**: Proves people want this, but no one is selling it as a productized solution

---

## Conclusion: The Gap Holds

**No product or service currently offers a recurring weekly planning session where two people sit down together to fill in one shared page integrating home and work as its primary deliverable.**

What exists:
- ✅ Apps with weekly check-ins for home/relationship (Fair Play, FairShare, Haklo)
- ✅ Apps with weekly check-ins for relationship quality (Paired, Kindred, Connected)
- ✅ Shared calendar/task apps (Cozi, Ohai, Domus, TimeTree, etc.)
- ✅ Individual work planning apps with weekly reviews (Sunsama, Akiflow)
- ✅ Guides and templates for DIY weekly meetings (Notion, Google Docs)

What doesn't exist:
- ❌ A product where the weekly session (both people, ~30 minutes, one shared artifact, home + work) is the core offering
- ❌ The session as the product (not just a feature)
- ❌ Built-in facilitation for the integrated weekly planning ritual

**Verdict: Gap holds.**

The closest partial fills are **Tandem** (couples app with Weekly Sync, but home-focused) and **Huddle** (positions the weekly meeting, but waitlist-only and details unclear). Neither explicitly positions or demonstrates "two people filling in one shared weekly page covering home and work."
